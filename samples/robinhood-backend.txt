As a Software Developer Intern, Backend, you won't be on the sidelines — you'll own a meaningful project end-to-end, from initial scoping through to production. You'll be embedded on a real team, working directly with experienced developers, diving deep into how the US financial system actually operates, and shipping software that meets the correctness and scale requirements of a live financial platform. This is a summer where you'll leave with real impact to show for it!
This role is based in our Toronto, ON office, with in-person attendance expected at least 3 days per week.
At Robinhood, we believe in the power of in-person work to accelerate progress, spark innovation, and strengthen community. Our office experience is intentional, energizing, and designed to fully support high-performing teams.
What you'll do
- Own a high-impact backend project from initial scoping through launch — you'll drive the work, not just support it
- Partner with a dedicated mentor who will guide you from onboarding through shipping, deepening your understanding of our tech stack and financial domain along the way
- Design, build, test, and release software solutions that meet the correctness and scalability requirements of a real financial platform
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams across Robinhood to solve critical problems that span the organization
- Maintain and scale an existing codebase using technologies such as Python/Django, Go, PostgreSQL, Kafka, Redis, Memcached, AWS, and Kubernetes
What you bring
- Currently enrolled in a full-time, degree-seeking program with an expected graduation date of Winter 2027 or Spring 2028
- Strong CS fundamentals and deep understanding of best coding practices
- Solid communication skills and an ability to drive large projects forward in a fast-paced, sometimes ambiguous environment
- Passion and interest in financial technology and building fintech products and services
- Prior software development experience from internships, hackathons, or personal projects
- Familiarity with Python/Django or Go, and an understanding of relational databases and transactions
